What the goal takes

Lead source split

Your target share of closes from each source, against what's happening this month. Aim for at least 3–4 sources with activity so no single one can sink your month.

Daily time split

  • Leads flowing: about 5 hours on new leads, 2 hours on referrals, follow-ups and CRM farming.
  • Leads drying up: spread the day, about 1 hour on each other source, still an 8-hour day.
  • Keep every source running a little, even in good weeks. Starting one from zero when you need it is slow.
